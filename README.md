# ECHO ARENA
### Four stages. Six fighters. One tower.

**ECHO ARENA** is a fast-paced first-person anime brawler made in **Godot 4.7**.
A rogue AI called **the Warden** has seized the arena district of Neo-Kairo and flooded it
with combat robots. Pick your fighter, smash through four neon stages, and take down the
Warden in a two-phase boss battle at the top of his tower.

## Fighters
| Fighter | Style | Signature Ultimate |
|---|---|---|
| **VOLT** | Energy: rapid bolts and a charged piercing beam | **STARFALL**: bolts rain from the sky |
| **KAGE** | Sword: fast blade combos and a dash-slash | **THOUSAND CUTS**: time stops, everything gets cut |
| **RIOT** | Fists & kicks: combos and a ground-shaking meteor dive | **RUSH MODE**: unstoppable flurry |
| **HEX** | Magic: homing orbs, freezing sigils, blink | **METEOR RAIN**: a giant sky circle drops meteors |
| **NOVA** | Gunslinger: twin pistols, shotgun blast, stun grenade | **SOLAR BARRAGE**: homing lock-on volley |
| **JADE** | Wind spear: long-reach combos and a piercing javelin | **TEMPEST**: a roaming tornado |

## Combat
- **Style Rank (D to SSS):** chain hits to climb from DECENT to UNSTOPPABLE. Get hit and you drop.
- **Block & Parry:** raise a hex energy shield, and time it right to reflect bullets and stun robots.
- **Perfect Dodge:** dash through an attack at the last second to slow down time.
- **Ultimates:** fill your meter by fighting, then unleash a screen-filling finisher.

## Stages
1. **Neon Streets:** melee drones
2. **Rooftops:** gunners in the rain
3. **Reactor Core:** shielded walkers
4. **The Warden's Tower:** a two-phase boss: a giant mech, then a flying core
